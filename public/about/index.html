About

A research institute alongside a K-12 classical Christian education school.

Spring Vale Christian Education Institute is a research initiative of Spring Vale Christian School, a Kindergarten through Grade 12 classical Christian boarding school in Owosso, Michigan, teaching since 1946.

Why the Institute exists

The subject had no continuous record.

Christian schooling in the United States is discussed constantly and measured rarely. The federal census counts schools every two years. Enrollment figures arrive late and in fragments. Nobody was keeping a continuous, comparable, month-by-month record of where the country stood.

The Institute keeps that record. It runs from January 2004 to the present, it covers every state in every month, and it is published in full.

How we work

Publish the measure, the method, and the data.

Every figure carries the month it was measured in. Every count carries its source and its edition. The measurement is described completely enough that a reader who disagrees with a conclusion can find the step they disagree with, and the data are published so that they can recompute it.

Where we have been wrong, we say so, with a date, and every document the figure appeared in is rebuilt.

Independence

What the Institute will not do.

The Institute is non-partisan. It takes no position on school choice legislation, it endorses no candidate, and no party, and it accepts no funding conditioned on a finding. It does not attribute a cause to a movement it has not measured the cause of, and it does not describe a relative position as a quantity.

Correct use

Everything the Institute publishes is released under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. You may copy it, redistribute it, and build on it for any purpose, including commercially, provided you meet the four conditions below.

Attribute
Credit the Spring Vale Christian Education Institute, name what you are citing, and link to it.
Cite the month with the data
Every index value belongs to the month it was measured in. A figure reproduced without its month is not our figure.
Never turn a position into a count
The indices measure relative position: where a state stands against the other forty-nine, and against its own record. They are not counts of children, schools, or enrollments, and a movement in an index is a change in standing rather than growth or decline in any quantity.
Mark what you changed
Adapted material must be identified as adapted, under section 3(a)(1)(B) of the license. Where an adaptation misstates what the indices measure, or presents altered figures as the Institute’s own, the Institute will exercise its right under section 3(a)(3) to require that attribution to the Institute be removed from it.
