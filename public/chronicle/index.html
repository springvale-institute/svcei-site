The Chronicle

Our measurements and public policy, on one timeline.

Choose an index, press play, and watch the country from January 2004 to the present. The Policy Monitor’s dated events sit on the same timeline, at their own dates.

What this feature can and cannot tell you

It was built to answer a question it cannot answer: which policies moved the indices. No arrangement of these two datasets answers that, and a page that appeared to would be the one thing the Institute has said it will not do.

What it does instead is put the measurement and the instrument on one timeline, each at its own date, and let a reader see what happened near what. A ruling and a movement in the same month may be connected, may be connected through something neither of them names, or may be one coincidence out of the two hundred and seventy-one months in this series. The page shows the coincidence in time, says that is what it is, and stops there.

No event is ever drawn as a cause. Nothing is highlighted for sitting near a movement. Events are placed at their recorded dates and never at a date chosen to line up with anything. The Institute reports the movement and does not explain it, though we posit how and why it occurred in our research.

How to read it

The map is the standing of every state on the index you chose, in the month the slider is at. Click a state, or choose one, to follow it: its own line appears below with the same events beneath it. The marks on the timeline are Policy Monitor entries — blue for federal, dark red for state. Click any mark to jump the playhead to that month and read the entry, with its evidence tier and, where a court published one, its vote.

An empty stretch means the inventory, not the country

The Policy Monitor’s fifty-one jurisdiction inventory is being assembled jurisdiction by jurisdiction. A month with no event recorded in it means nothing has been sourced for that month yet. It does not mean nothing happened, and the page says so rather than letting a gap be read as quiet.

Correct use

Everything the Institute publishes is released under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. You may copy it, redistribute it, and build on it for any purpose, including commercially, provided you meet the four conditions below.

Attribute
Credit the Spring Vale Christian Education Institute, name what you are citing, and link to it.
Cite the month with the data
Every index value belongs to the month it was measured in. A figure reproduced without its month is not our figure.
Never turn a position into a count
The indices measure relative position: where a state stands against the other forty-nine, and against its own record. They are not counts of children, schools, or enrollments, and a movement in an index is a change in standing rather than growth or decline in any quantity.
Mark what you changed
Adapted material must be identified as adapted, under section 3(a)(1)(B) of the license. Where an adaptation misstates what the indices measure, or presents altered figures as the Institute’s own, the Institute will exercise its right under section 3(a)(3) to require that attribution to the Institute be removed from it.
