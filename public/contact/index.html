Whether you run a school, lead a congregation, write for a newspaper, or simply wondered about a number you saw here, your message reaches a real inbox and a real person reads it. Questions are welcome, corrections especially so, and no question about this work is too small to ask.

What happens next

So that nobody is left wondering.

A person reads every message here. Most get an answer within a day. Should yours simply be a kind word, we will read it gladly and expect nothing of you in return.

You saw a number and want to understand it

Ask. This is the most welcome message we receive, and the answer will carry the figure together with the month it was true in. Nobody here thinks less of a reader for asking what a rank means.

You think something is wrong

Tell us, and thank you. A published figure that is wrong gets checked against the series and corrected, and the correction carries the date it was made. We would far rather hear it from you than not hear it.

You want to use the work

Please do. Everything here is free to reuse under a Creative Commons Attribution license, the charts may be embedded, and you do not need our permission first. Write only if something is unclear or you need a format we have not published.

You run a school or a church

We would like to hear what the record looks like from where you sit, and whether it matches what you see. That kind of message shapes what gets measured next.

One thing we will not do, and it is better said plainly: we do not explain why a state moved. The record shows movement, and attributing a cause would be an opinion wearing the clothes of a measurement. Ask us what moved, how far, and in which month, and you will get a straight answer.

Your name, your email address, and your message go to our intake provider and into the Institute’s own inbox. They are used to write back to you, are never sold, are never shared, and are not added to any mailing list. The newsletter is a separate choice, and it is yours to make.

Correct use

Everything the Institute publishes is released under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. You may copy it, redistribute it, and build on it for any purpose, including commercially, provided you meet the four conditions below.

Attribute
Credit the Spring Vale Christian Education Institute, name what you are citing, and link to it.
Cite the month with the data
Every index value belongs to the month it was measured in. A figure reproduced without its month is not our figure.
Never turn a position into a count
The indices measure relative position: where a state stands against the other forty-nine, and against its own record. They are not counts of children, schools, or enrollments, and a movement in an index is a change in standing rather than growth or decline in any quantity.
Mark what you changed
Adapted material must be identified as adapted, under section 3(a)(1)(B) of the license. Where an adaptation misstates what the indices measure, or presents altered figures as the Institute’s own, the Institute will exercise its right under section 3(a)(3) to require that attribution to the Institute be removed from it.
