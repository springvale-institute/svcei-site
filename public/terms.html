Correct use

Attribution alone does not protect a measure. Someone can credit us exactly as asked and still print an index value with no month attached to it, or write that a state's Christian schooling "grew 4%" because a relative position moved four points. Both are misuse, both look like citation, and neither is something we can fix after the fact. So the four conditions below travel with the license, on every report as well as here.

Attribute. Credit the Spring Vale Christian Education Institute, name the report, and link to it. Every report carries three ready formats in its citation block.

Carry the month. Every index value belongs to the month it was measured in. A figure reproduced without its month is not our figure.

Never turn a position into a count. The indices measure relative position: where a state stands against the other forty-nine, and against its own record. They are not counts of children, schools, or enrollments, and a movement in an index is a change in standing rather than growth or decline in any quantity.

Mark what you changed. Adapted material must be identified as adapted, under section 3(a)(1)(B) of the license.

Where an adaptation misstates what the indices measure, or presents altered figures as ours, we will exercise our right under section 3(a)(3) of the license to require that attribution to the Institute be removed from it. That is the remedy the license gives a licensor, it is the one we will use, and using it is not a withdrawal of the license: the work stays free to reuse, correctly.

What the index measures, and what it does not

The index measures public attention to Christian education. It does not measure enrollment. A rising index does not mean more students, and must not be reported as though it does. Capacity figures come from a federal survey with its own reference period and lag, and describe the Christian schooling that exists rather than demand, quality, or need.

Figures resting on population estimates not yet finalized are marked provisional and will be restated on a published schedule. Our methodology, including every decision that shapes the numbers, is published in full.
