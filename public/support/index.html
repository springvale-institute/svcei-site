Support

Keep the record going.

The Institute publishes everything it produces, free, under an open license. Our research is made possible through the support of generous donors who are interested in Christian education. Please consider becoming a donor to sustain our mission and research.

What support pays for

Three things.

One

The reports

More than 1,100 volumes a year researched and published across eight formats, each one built from current data.

Two

The checks

Every volume put through eight checks before it publishes, and held back rather than published if it fails one.

Three

Open publication

Publishing the measure, the method, and the data, so that anyone can check the arithmetic and build on the work.

To discuss supporting the Institute, contact us via our website or call us at the number on this website.
