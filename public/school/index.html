Tell us about your Christian school

The census counts you. It does not know you.

The federal private school census records a name, a city, a level, and an enrollment. It does not record what a school is like. This form is where a school tells the Institute the rest.

What happens to what you send

What you send here is for the Institute's own research and correspondence. It is not published. It is not shared. It is not sold. It does not enter any index, any count, or any figure the Institute publishes. If the Institute ever wants to publish something you have told it, it will ask you first, in writing, for that specific thing.

The Institute measures two things and publishes them: Attention, which is how strongly a place is oriented toward Christian schooling, and Schools, which is the federal census of Christian schools. What a school says about itself is a third kind of thing. A school describing itself is not a federal count, and if it ever entered one the Institute would have quietly changed what it measures and told nobody. So it never does.

What you get back

The Christian Education Market Area your address falls in, how much Christian schooling that area provides, and where your state stands. All of it is drawn from figures the Institute has already published, so nothing you have just typed is used to build it. You can see the same card without filling anything in, at the market area finder.

Before you send

Thank you — that reached us.

A person at the Institute reads every one of these. What you have sent goes to the Register, which is internal: it is used for the Institute’s own research and correspondence and is not published against a named school or congregation without your written permission.

If something needs checking we will write to the address you gave. If it does not, you will not hear from us, and nothing you sent is passed to anyone else.

Why enrollment is a band

Three reasons, and the third is the one administrators raise first. A band cannot be mistaken for a census figure. A band is far likelier to be answered honestly by a school that would not publish an exact number. A band cannot be assembled by a competitor into a market intelligence file.

Correct use

Everything the Institute publishes is released under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. You may copy it, redistribute it, and build on it for any purpose, including commercially, provided you meet the four conditions below.

Attribute
Credit the Spring Vale Christian Education Institute, name what you are citing, and link to it.
Cite the month with the data
Every index value belongs to the month it was measured in. A figure reproduced without its month is not our figure.
Never turn a position into a count
The indices measure relative position: where a state stands against the other forty-nine, and against its own record. They are not counts of children, schools, or enrollments, and a movement in an index is a change in standing rather than growth or decline in any quantity.
Mark what you changed
Adapted material must be identified as adapted, under section 3(a)(1)(B) of the license. Where an adaptation misstates what the indices measure, or presents altered figures as the Institute’s own, the Institute will exercise its right under section 3(a)(3) to require that attribution to the Institute be removed from it.
